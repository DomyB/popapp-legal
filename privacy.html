PopApp Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026

PopApp ("we", "the app") helps international students discover events, places, clubs, and each other. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We keep it short because we collect little.

What we collect

  • Account basics: the name you enter, your university, and your nationality. Email is optional (only if you sign in with an email code).
  • Content you create: profile photo, bio, interests, club videos, chat messages, events you publish, saved items, and RSVPs.
  • Safety records: reports you submit and users you block.

Location

With your permission, your device's approximate coordinates are used to find nearby events and places. Coordinates are sent to the data providers listed below to run those searches. We do not store your location history on our servers. You can use the app with a manually chosen city instead.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • No third-party analytics profiling.
  • We never read your private messages except when a message is reported for moderation.

Where data lives

Account data and content are stored with Supabase (hosted in the United States), protected by row-level security so users can only access what they're authorized to see.

Third-party data sources

Event and place results come from providers including Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, Google Places, the U.S. National Park Service, Dice, Luma, RunSignup, parkrun, university and museum public calendars, and neighborhood organization calendars. Your search coordinates and queries are shared with these providers to fetch results; their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your profile, messages, videos, memberships, saved items, and RSVPs. Anonymous (name-only) accounts cannot be recovered after sign-out or deletion. You can also email us for deletion or a copy of your data.

Age

PopApp is intended for users 17 and older.
